Photo / Document bhejein

Aapki photo ya file seedha shop ke computer par pahunchegi.

Passport Photo ke liye shop ka Photo wala QR scan karein, tabhi ye section chalega.

Photo chunne ke baad yahan dikhegi.
Chehra seedha aur saaf ho.

Photo phone par hi lock (encrypt) hoti hai, isliye sirf is shop ka computer ise khol sakta hai.

Documents ke liye shop ka WhatsApp Print wala QR scan karein, tabhi ye section chalega.

Chuni hui photo / files yahan dikhengi.
ID card ke liye pehle Front, phir Back chunein.

Sab kuch phone par hi lock (encrypt) hota hai, isliye sirf is shop ka computer ise khol sakta hai. Ek file 14 MB tak, ek baar mein 40 files tak.

Ye QR kis ke liye hai?

Is QR mein tool ka naam nahi hai. Jis computer tool se QR bana hai, wo chunein.
